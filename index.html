What we grow

Everything comes off our smallholding here in Honiton. What's in your box depends entirely on what's ready that week. We don't buy anything in.

  • Potatoes — Pentland Javelin, Charlotte, Cara
  • Carrots — Early Nantes
  • Brassicas — cavolo nero, kale, savoy cabbage, sprouting broccoli
  • Salad — mixed leaves, rocket, spring onions
  • Onions — red and brown, shallots
  • Beetroot — Boltardy
  • Leeks, swede, parsnips (autumn into winter)
  • French beans, courgettes, squash (summer)
  • Apples —自家的 Bramley and Katy (Sept–Nov)
  • Eggs — from our free-range hens
